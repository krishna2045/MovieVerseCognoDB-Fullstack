MovieVerse UI/UX Case Study

An award-winning, Apple-level UI presentation for an AI-powered cinematic discovery platform. Built with Dark Mode, Glassmorphism, 18px border-radius, and custom Neon accents.

Color Tokens: #090B14 (Background) #8B5CF6 (Purple Neon) #3B82F6 (Blue Glow) #EC4899 (Pink Accent)

Login & Authentication Flow

Left Panel Specification
  • Brand Logo: Pink "Movie" + Blue Gradient "Verse"
  • Heading & Tagline: "Welcome Back" with cinematic subtitle
  • Floating Illustrations: Film Reel, Popcorn Bucket, Clapperboard, Cinema Tickets with glowing purple smoke
  • Glass Cards: 4 Feature Cards (Discover Movies, Personalized Recommendations, Explore Actors, Knowledge Graph)
Right Panel & OAuth Flow
  • Login Card: Glassmorphism 28px radius, purple neon border, soft blur
  • Input Controls: Email (Envelope icon), Password (Lock & Eye toggle), Remember Me & Forgot Password
  • Action Button: Gradient Purple → Blue with soft glow shadow
  • Social Login: Official Google, GitHub, and Apple OAuth buttons
  • Flow Transition: Animated arrow indicating login success → redirect to Dashboard
